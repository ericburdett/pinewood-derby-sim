Part 1 · How does the AI work?

An LLM is a next-word predictor

It's like the autocomplete on your phone keyboard — but trained on a huge chunk of the internet.

The fastest pinewood car is → heavy

Part 1 · How does the AI work?

It guesses the most likely next word

For "The fastest pinewood car is ___" it ranks every possible word:

heavy
61%
fast
22%
red
9%

Pick a word, add it, then predict the next one. Repeat. That's it — one word at a time.

Part 1 · How does the AI work?

Why does it sound helpful?

Raw prediction just sounds like the internet. So humans rate thousands of answers — 👍 good vs 👎 bad — and the AI is re-trained to give answers people prefer.

This is called RLHF

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback. It learns to be helpful, honest, and polite — by copying what humans thumbs-up.

Part 1 · The catch

It predicts words — it doesn't "know" things

That's why it can sound confident and still be wrong. It's a brilliant guesser, not an oracle.

Which is exactly why the next part matters...

Part 2 · From chatbot to agent

What's an agent?

A chatbot just talks. An agent can act — it's given tools and runs in a loop, deciding its own next step.

🎯 Goal → 🤔 Decide → 🔧 Use a tool → 👀 See result ↺

Loop until the job is done.

Part 2 · From chatbot to agent

Claude Code is an agent for building software

Its tools are a programmer's tools. It decides which to use, when:

📖 Read & search

Looks through the project's files to understand the code.

✍️ Write code

Creates and edits files to add features or fix bugs.

▶️ Run commands

Runs the tests and the app to check its own work.

🔁 Fix & repeat

Sees errors, decides a fix, tries again — on its own.

Part 3 · The app we'll race in

The Pinewood Derby Simulator

  • Design a car with sliders — weight, shape, wheels, axles
  • Real physics engine runs the race (friction, drag, gravity)
  • Watch it race and see where your speed went
  • A "Learn" tab teaches what actually makes cars fast

Cool part: the physics is written in Python, running inside your web browser. The AI wired all that together.
